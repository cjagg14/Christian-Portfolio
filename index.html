Space Systems Design — SOOS-1

An autonomous satellite-servicing spacecraft designed to rendezvous with five defunct GEO satellites, dock via a universal engine-bell capture mechanism, and either restore them to operational orbit or passivate them into a graveyard orbit. My contributions include orbital path determination and STK simulations, ΔV and fuel optimisation in MATLAB, and producing the full mission animation in Blender.
